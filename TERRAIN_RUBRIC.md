# Terrain User App — High-Level Project Rubric

## Product Vision

Terrain is a holistic fitness and wellness app for people who are deeply invested in their health and are striving to reach and maintain peak levels of wellness, fitness, and overall health. It brings personalized training, nutrition, sleep, recovery, daily activity, and wellness signals into one guided view so users can understand their full health picture and act on it.

A personalized fitness plan is a core part of Terrain, but it is not the whole product. Terrain connects the plan with how the user eats, sleeps, recovers, feels, and moves through daily life. Nora, Terrain's AI Coach, interprets that complete picture and provides personalized guidance, while the user contributes the real-world information needed to keep the guidance relevant.

**Nora:** Nora is Terrain's sole AI Coach for MVP. As a well-defined AI coaching agent, Nora must take on all planning and adaptation responsibilities previously assigned to a human coach while preserving the same safety, personalization, and communication standards.

## Primary Product Outcome

Terrain delivers a **holistic and guided view of wellness**. A successful end product helps a highly health-motivated user understand how their training, nutrition, sleep, recovery, activity, and daily habits work together—and what they can do to continue moving toward their personal peak.

The user should always understand:

- Their overall wellness and readiness today, including the factors influencing it.
- How their fitness plan, nutrition, sleep, recovery, and daily activity support their goals.
- What they can do today to maintain momentum or improve an area that needs attention.
- How their behaviors and results are changing over time.
- What Nora needs to know in order to provide useful guidance.

## Core Experience Rubric

### 1. Holistic Wellness Overview

**Outcome:** Terrain gives the user a coherent view of the factors shaping their wellness and progress toward peak health and fitness.

The end product meets the bar when:

- The user can track workouts, steps, meals, sleep, energy, soreness or pain, stress or mood, hydration, and body weight.
- These signals appear as parts of one wellness picture rather than as unrelated logs the user must interpret alone.
- Terrain combines the day’s relevant signals into a friendly readiness or wellness score with an accompanying emoji and a plain-language explanation.
- The user can notice meaningful patterns across training, nutrition, sleep, recovery, and daily habits over time.
- Meal tracking remains lightweight and supports snapping a meal photo to estimate food quantity and provide a basic nutrition profile.
- Food-photo results are presented as useful estimates, not precise measurements, and the user can correct them when needed.
- Wellness information is visible to Nora by default, while the user can keep individual sensitive entries private and can always understand what is being shared with her.

### 2. Personalized Fitness Plan

**Outcome:** The trainee never has to decide what body part to train or assemble a workout at the gym.

The end product meets the bar when:

- Every trainee has a fitness plan prescribed by Nora and aligned with their goals, ability, available time, preferences, equipment, and known limitations.
- The plan coordinates gym sessions with running, Pilates, yoga, pickleball, sprint training, and other activities the trainee enjoys.
- Recurring preferences—such as a weekly three-mile run—can shape the ongoing calendar so Nora avoids conflicting or overly intense training.
- The plan feels personal and responsive rather than like a generic workout library.

### 3. Fitness Calendar

**Outcome:** The calendar is the reliable source of truth for planned and completed physical activity.

The end product meets the bar when:

- Workouts and activities prescribed by Nora appear in a clear fitness calendar.
- A prescribed activity remains pending until the trainee records what actually happened; scheduling alone never counts as completion.
- The trainee can add ad-hoc workouts and activities from outside the prescribed plan.
- The trainee can report conflicts, reschedule activities, and request changes without losing Nora’s intent.
- Planned, completed, partially completed, skipped, and trainee-added activities are easy to distinguish.

### 4. Guided Gym Workout Experience

**Outcome:** Terrain acts as an in-gym companion that helps the trainee use their available time optimally and safely.

The end product meets the bar when:

- The prescribed workout should be created with the user's goals in mind.
- Every guided gym session includes a warm-up, main workout, and cooldown.
- The trainee can see the exercises, prescribed weight, sets, repetitions, and relevant instructions from Nora throughout the session.
- Exercise guidance includes links to useful YouTube demonstrations when the trainee needs a visual explanation.
- The experience is easy to follow while actively exercising and keeps the trainee focused on the next useful action.
- The trainee can record actual weights and repetitions, perceived difficulty, pain or limitations, and an optional note for Nora.

### 5. Guided Sprint Workout Experience

**Outcome:** Track-based sprint sessions receive guidance suited to sprint training rather than being treated like gym workouts.

The end product meets the bar when:

- A sprint session clearly guides the trainee through its longer warm-up, short max-effort sprint intervals, recovery periods, and cooldown walk or run.
- The trainee understands the purpose, effort, and safe execution of each stage.
- The completed session and trainee feedback become part of the same planning and adaptation history Nora uses for gym workouts.

### 6. Additional Activities

**Outcome:** Terrain plans around the trainee’s existing physical life instead of competing with it.

The end product meets the bar when:

- Runs, Pilates, yoga, pickleball, sprint-club sessions, padel and other opted-in activities can be scheduled and recorded.
- These activities receive a lighter experience than guided gym and sprint workouts while still contributing to the calendar, Nora’s planning context, progress, and daily wellness picture.
- Nora and the trainee can coordinate preferences so enjoyable activities remain part of a balanced plan.

### 7. Nora AI Coaching, Communication, and Adaptation

**Outcome:** The trainee can maintain an ongoing relationship with Nora without information becoming scattered across unrelated channels.

The end product meets the bar when:

- The trainee can communicate with Nora through chat about progress, injuries, pain, schedule changes, preferences, and questions.
- The trainee can request substitutions or adjustments while Nora remains responsible for the plan’s overall direction.
- Nora uses workout results and wellness context to adapt future guidance.
- Important feedback does not disappear after a single conversation; it remains part of the persistent context Nora uses to plan and adapt guidance.

### 8. Progress and Personal Records

**Outcome:** The trainee can see meaningful evidence of progress across the gym, track, and running.

The end product meets the bar when:

- A dedicated Personal Records view supports self-reported lifting, running, and sprint achievements.
- Records preserve enough context to make the achievement meaningful, such as the activity, result, and date.
- Workout history and personal records make progress visible without reducing success to a single number.
- The trainee and Nora can celebrate progress and use it to inform future goals.

### 9. Trust, Safety, and Clarity

**Outcome:** Trainees can rely on Terrain and Nora for safe, clear fitness and wellness guidance.

The end product meets the bar when:

- Exercise guidance from Nora consistently prioritizes safe performance and respects known injuries or limitations.
- Nora may summarize patterns and support safer training choices, but she does not diagnose conditions, provide medical treatment, or imply professional clearance.
- When injuries, symptoms, nutrition issues, or health information raise concern, Nora directs the trainee toward an appropriate qualified professional.
- Guidance, scores, estimates, and uncertainty are communicated in plain, supportive, nonjudgmental language.

## End-Product Success Standard

Terrain succeeds as a complete trainee product when:

- The user can open Terrain and quickly understand their current wellness picture and the factors influencing it.
- Training, nutrition, sleep, recovery, activity, and daily habits feel connected rather than scattered across unrelated tools.
- Terrain helps the user recognize patterns and make informed choices in pursuit of peak wellness, fitness, and health.
- The trainee can open the app and confidently follow a relevant plan for the day.
- The guided workout experience is useful during a real 45-minute gym session or a track-based sprint session.
- The fitness calendar accurately reflects both Nora’s plan and the trainee’s actual physical life.
- The trainee’s feedback, preferences, injuries, wellness signals, and completed activities meaningfully inform Nora’s planning and adaptation.
- The trainee can recognize consistency and progress through workout history and personal records.
- Holistic guidance feels supportive and actionable rather than overwhelming, clinical, or judgmental.
- The major experiences work together as one continuous loop between the trainee and Nora: observe, understand, plan, perform, reflect, communicate, and adapt.

## Stretch Goals

- **Strava connection:** Automatically bring eligible runs into Terrain so the calendar, progress history, and Nora’s planning context stay current.
- **Apple Health and similar connections:** Incorporate relevant activity and wellness data without making a third-party connection necessary for the core Terrain experience.
